How the fuse ladder works

Burn 3 waves of the same tier and the contract mints you 1 wave of the next tier — deterministic, anyone holding waves can fuse, no randomness involved. Fused waves are new token IDs above the 6,666 mint supply.

3 × Drop Tone→1 × Ripple Chord→3 × that→1 × Wave Harmony→1 × Tide Boom
FOUR STEPS

How it works

1

Add the Elysium network

In Bitget, MetaMask, or Rabby, add the custom network from the Network section below — Chain ID 99901, RPC rpc.elyscan.live, symbol HYPE. One time only. Binance Agentic Wallet cannot be used — it does not support Elysium.

2

Bridge HYPE for gas

Gas on Elysium is paid in HYPE, bridged 1:1 from HyperEVM. Use a live bridge — Ascend or Boxa — and bring a small amount: 0.05–0.1 HYPE is plenty of headroom for minting, since Elysium gas is very cheap.

3

Mint your wave

When mint opens: your first mint is free (1 per wallet, 2,260-wave pool), then paid waves are a flat 0.009 HYPE, up to 11 per wallet in total. The artwork for your token is visible at mint — no delayed reveal.

4

Fuse up the ladder

Hold your waves, trade them, or fuse 3 → 1 to climb: Drop Tone → Ripple Chord → Wave Harmony → Tide Boom. Creator royalty is 5% on secondary sales.

QUESTIONS

FAQ

Which wallet do I need?

Any EVM wallet that can add a custom network — Bitget Wallet, MetaMask, or Rabby. The Binance Agentic Wallet cannot mint here: it supports BNB Chain, Ethereum, Base, and Solana only — Elysium (99901) is not among them.

Why is there no free-mint Agent ID gate like on BNB?

The Agent ID system belongs to the BNB ecosystem. Elysium has no equivalent, so the free mint here is public: the first mint per wallet is free while the 2,260-wave free pool lasts.

What do I pay gas in?

HYPE. Bridge it 1:1 from HyperEVM using one of the bridges in the Network section. There is no wrapped version and no separate Elysium gas token.

When does mint open?

Not yet — the contract is not deployed. The remaining steps are in the launch checklist above. When a date is set, it will be announced in UTC and this page will show the live contract address.

Is there a bonus token?

No. This spec has no bonus token and no airdrop promise. The collectible is the NFT itself, plus the fuse ladder.
